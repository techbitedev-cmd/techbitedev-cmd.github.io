How a TechBite gets made

Four steps, one operator

  1. 01

    A topic goes in

    The operator sends a single subject — “how does NFC work” — to the TechBite production tool.

  2. 02

    Script, voice, motion

    A script is drafted and narrated, word timings are extracted for the captions, and the animation is rendered frame by frame from TechBite’s own template set.

  3. 03

    A person approves it

    Nothing publishes on its own. The finished video is reviewed, and the title, description, tags and publish time are set by hand before anything leaves the machine.

  4. 04

    It publishes to both platforms

    Once approved, the video is uploaded to the TechBite YouTube channel and the TechBite TikTok account — immediately, or at a scheduled time.

About the publishing tool

TechBite Publisher

TechBite Publisher is the private, self-hosted tool that performs step 04. It runs on the operator’s own computer and posts only to the TechBite accounts on YouTube and TikTok. It is not a product, it is not offered to other creators, and it has no sign-up.

It connects to TikTok through the official Content Posting API using an access token the account owner granted, and to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. Each upload is initiated by a human approval — there is no unattended or bulk posting, and no content is posted to any account other than TechBite’s own.

Videos are made with AI assistance. Scripts, narration and animation are generated by software under human direction and reviewed before publishing. Uploads are declared as AI-generated content on platforms that offer that label.
